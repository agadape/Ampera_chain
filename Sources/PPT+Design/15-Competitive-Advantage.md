## Purpose
PLN's mandatory competitive-advantage frame. Show the landscape honestly, then prove Ampera's moat is structural — not a feature, but a combination no competitor holds: multi-energy + financing-native + AI site-selection + carbon, in one platform. Pair the 2×2 matrix with a direct comparison table (per PLN hint: "by table or diagram") so the advantage is scannable, not asserted.
