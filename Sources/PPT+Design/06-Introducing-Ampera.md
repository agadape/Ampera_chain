# Slide 06 — Introducing Ampera · One Bridge, Three Pillars

> Archetype D — Pillar diagram (70%). Lead: green. Light slide.

---

## Purpose
Pivot from problem to solution. Introduce Ampera as a single platform with three distinct pillars — energy-agnostic generation, ESG/carbon accounting, and PLN-native partnership. The audience must understand *the shape* of the product before any detail.

## Headline (H2)
### Satu jembatan. Tiga pilar.

## Eyebrow
`SOLUSI · OVERVIEW`

## Body copy (text 30%, ≤55 words)
> Ampera bukan proyek panel surya. Ampera adalah **platform jembatan**: mengubah sumber energi lokal menjadi aset digital yang bisa didanai, diukur, dan dipercaya. Tiga pilar — **Flex, Earth, PLN** — bekerja sebagai satu sistem, dari sumber daya hingga kilowatt ke rumah.

## Data to visualize (pillar specs)
| Pillar | Tagline | Role | Key metric / fact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ampera Flex** | Energy-agnostic | Solar · piko-hidro · biomassa dalam satu sistem | Piko-hidro: CAPEX Rp2–5jt, 24/7, yield tertinggi |
| **Ampera Earth** | Setiap kilowatt diakui | ESG/SDG real-time + karbon digital | IDXCarbon Rp32.612/tCO₂e |
| **Ampera × PLN** | Dibangun bersama PLN | 4 lapis nilai: data, grid, finansial, kepemimpinan | Diesel saving Rp96–840M / 100 desa |

## Visual composition (70%)
- **Hero object (70%):** a dense **isometric "bridge & pillars" landscape** — not 3 big empty boxes, but a living scene with many small objects per pillar. Reads bottom→top as "shared bridge deck → 3 populated pillars → bridge arc overhead."
- **Environment backdrop (full zone):** paper `#F6F7ED` with a **dense topographic contour motif** (§5.3, `#00804C` @ 8%) + a faint Nusantara archipelago ghost at 6% in the lower band. Ambient: 2-3 tiny cloud glyphs (4px, `#1E488F` @ 30%) drifting upper margin, a faint compass rose (8px) corner. This is a landscape, not a blank canvas.
- **Base — "bridge deck" (bottom ≈15% of zone, full width):** a thick `#001F3F` deck bar (5px) with the `ampera` wordmark node (lime, §5.1) centered on it. Along the deck, ~6 small **`$AMP` token dots** (3px, violet-ringed) drift slowly left→right = "energy flowing through the platform." 3 thin lime flow-connectors (§5.6) rise from the deck up into each pillar = "the platform feeds the pillars."
- **Three pillars (center ≈65% of zone height, 3 columns):** NOT single big cards — each pillar is a **stacked cluster of 3-4 small component chips** rising from the deck:
  - **Pillar 1 — Flex** (`#00804C→#74C365` gradient cap, top): cap holds name + sun+water+leaf composite icon (28px). Below the cap, 3 small **component chips** (rounded, 60×40px each, white plate + green top border): `☀️ surya` · `💧 piko-hidro` (lime-ringed, hero) · `🌿 biomassa` — each with a 1-word metric micro-label (`Rp14-18jt` / `24/7` / `sawit`). The pillar is a *stack of energy options*, not one block.
  - **Pillar 2 — Earth** (`#74C365→#1E488F` cap): cap + leaf+down-arrow icon (§5.12). Below: 3 small chips: `ukur emisi` · `konversi karbon` · `lapor ESG/SDG` — each with a micro-metric (`AMS-I.L` / `Rp32.612` / `SDG 7·13`). 
  - **Pillar 3 — PLN** (`#1E488F→#001F3F` cap): cap + grid-node-cluster icon. Below: 4 small chips: `data` · `grid offtaker` · `finansial` · `kepemimpinan` — each with micro-metric (`10.068 lokasi` / `lisensi PLN` / `Rp96-840M` / `EBT`). The densest pillar = the partnership has the most layers.
  - Each chip has a small lime number dot (§5.2) = the pillar's function index.
- **Inter-pillar flow:** thin 1px `#1E488F` connectors weave between adjacent pillar chips (data flows Flex→Earth→PLN) = "the 3 pillars are one system, not silos." A faint violet `#7D39EB` @ 20% thread runs through all 3 pillars = the `$AMP` audit-layer spine (fintech signal, subtle).
- **Top — "bridge arc" (overhead ≈15% of zone):** a single bold lime `#C6FF33` arc (3px, @ 80%) spanning over all 3 pillars = the bridge metaphor. At the arc apex, a small tagline node (lime, 8px) labeled *"satu sistem"* = "three pillars, one bridge." 3 small lime energy-dots (6px, glow) hang from the arc, one above each pillar cap = "each pillar energized."
- **Ambient detail (the "ramai" factor):** faint contour lines, ~6 drifting `$AMP` dots on the deck, 2 small bird glyphs in the upper margin, the violet audit-spine thread weaving through pillars. Small, many, alive.
- Background: paper `#F6F7ED` + contour motif dense.
