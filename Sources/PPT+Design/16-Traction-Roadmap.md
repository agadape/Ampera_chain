# Slide 16 — Traction & Roadmap · From 1 Village to 1,000

> Archetype D — Roadmap timeline (70%). Lead: green. Light slide.

---

## Purpose
Show credible, staged progression — not a fantasy leap. From validated tech (today) → 1 pilot village (Q4 2026) → 100 villages (2027) → 1,000 villages (2028–29). Align milestones with national programs (Lisdes, Indonesia Terang) so scale is credible, not aspirational hand-waving.

## Headline (H2)
### Dari satu desa, menuju seribu.

## Eyebrow
`TRACTION · ROADMAP`

## Body copy (text 30%, ≤55 words)
> Teknologi tervalidasi (F1 87–93%, mAP50 93,6%). Sekarang menuju **pilot 1 desa piko-hidro** di kuartal keempat 2026. Lalu 100 desa di 2027, 1.000 desa di 2028–29, selaras **Lisdes** & **Indonesia Terang**. Setiap milestone punya **indikator keberhasilan** terverifikasi — bukan janji. Skala menyusul bukti.

**B. Indikator keberhasilan (KPI per fase) + proyeksi pertumbuhan:**

## Visual composition (70%)
- **Hero object:** a horizontal **roadmap track**, center-right, 70% zone.
- A 4 px `#00804C` baseline; 5 milestone nodes (lime dots, glowing) at uneven spacing matching real timing (P0 left-dense, P4 far right).
- Each node: a vertical card above OR below the line (alternating, to avoid clutter) with phase, timing, milestone, gate.
- **P1 node emphasized** (lime ring) — the immediate next milestone, the pilot that proves the model.
- **Growth curve overlay (multi-metrik):** 3 faint area-chart lines rising P0→P4 behind the milestones — `#74C365` @ 15% (# desa: 1 → 10.000+), `#1E488F` @ 20% (revenue Rp/thn, log scale: 0 → ~Rp103,5M), `#C6FF33` @ 18% (tCO₂e/thn: 3,6 → 36.000+). Each line labeled at its right end. Makes the exponential shape visible across impact AND revenue, not just village count.
- **KPI mini-table strip** (compact, under the timeline): the 5-metric × 5-phase matrix (§B above) as small chips so each milestone card carries its own success indicator row.
- Background: paper + contour motif.
