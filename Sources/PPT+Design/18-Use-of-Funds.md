# Slide 18 — Use of Funds · 50 Juta, Satu Desa Terbukti

> Archetype E+ — Budget breakdown bars + pilot outcome (70% visual). Lead: midnight + lime. Light slide with dark outcome panel.

---

## Purpose
PLN's mandatory "Penggunaan Dana" slide. Show exactly how the Rp50 juta prize becomes one proven pilot village — not a vague "operasional." Every rupiah mapped to a deliverable that de-risks the next funding gate. Pair the line-item budget with the concrete outcome: by end of Q1 2027, 1 piko-hidro village live, platform data+carbon running, carbon credit pipeline open. This is the bridge between the prize and the SOM.
