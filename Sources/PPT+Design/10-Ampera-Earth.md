# Slide 10 — Ampera Earth · Every Kilowatt, Accounted

> Archetype D+ — Carbon lifecycle + community benefit (70%). Lead: green. Light slide.

---

## Purpose
Show the ESG/SDG + carbon layer: every kilowatt Ampera generates is automatically measured, converted to a verified carbon credit, and reported. Pair the technical lifecycle with the human outcome — carbon revenue flows back to the village community, making ecology an income source, not a reporting burden. Inclusive: the community is a beneficiary, not a backdrop.

## Headline (H2)
### Setiap kilowatt punya jejak. Kami hitung otomatis.

## Eyebrow
`PILAR · EARTH`

## Body copy (text 30%, ≤35 words)
> Bukan klaim hijau — bukti hijau. EBT gantikan diesel → **emisi dihindarkan** → jadi **kredit karbon** (AMS-I.L, IDXCarbon) → dilaporkan ESG/SDG. Pendapatan kembali ke desa. Piko-hidro ~**Rp387.700/kW/thn**.

## Visual composition (70%)
- **Hero object (70%):** a dense **"carbon lifecycle with community at its heart"** composition — not a split (cycle left + photo right), but one integrated circular system where the village kitchen photo sits at the CENTER of the lifecycle ring, making the community literally inside the carbon economy. Ambient: 2-3 tiny leaf glyphs (4px, `#00804C` @ 40%) drifting, a faint compass rose (8px) corner. All-green palette throughout (Earth slide rule).
