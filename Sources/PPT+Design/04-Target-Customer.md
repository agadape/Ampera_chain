# Slide 04 — Target Customer · Siapa yang Gelap, Siapa yang Bayar

> Archetype C+ — Customer triad + pain-point rail (70% visual). Lead: green→blue. Light slide.

---

## Purpose
PLN requires a dedicated customer & pain-points frame, separate from the market sizing. Make explicit *who* Ampera serves — there are three customer types, not one — and name the unmet need + pain point for each, so the jury sees the solution is demand-validated, not supply-driven. Clarify the B2B/B2G/B2C relationship model for every customer so the jury sees exactly how Ampera interfaces with each. This slide is the *full customer map*: desa 3T (penerima manfaat sekaligus agen lokal), PLN (off-taker & pemilik grid utama), dan investor — dua tier, dari warga mikro (mulai Rp2rb) hingga institusi global (likuiditas on-chain).

## Headline (H2)
### Tiga pelanggan. Satu kebutuhan: kilowatt yang terjangkau dan terpercaya.

## Eyebrow
`CUSTOMER · PAIN POINTS`

## Body copy (text 30%, ≤55 words)
> Ampera melayani tiga pelanggan sekaligus dengan model hubungan berbeda: **desa 3T** (B2C/B2G — penerima manfaat sekaligus agen lokal), **PLN** (B2B/B2G — off-taker & pemilik grid utama), dan **investor** — dua tier: **mikro warga** (B2C, OJK ECF/AKD via Wadah Bersama, mulai Rp2rb) dan **institusi global** (B2B, likuiditas & settlement on-chain, Fase 2). Tiap pelanggan punya pain point berbeda — diesel mahal, last-mile merugi, akses modal desa terbatas. Satu platform menjawab ketiganya.

## Visual composition (70%)
- **Hero object (70%):** a **triad layout** filling the full visual zone — the complete customer map (desa · PLN · investor), with the investor column carrying both tiers (mikro & global) so the jury sees the full demand side in one frame.
- **Three customer KPI-card columns (§5.9)** side by side, each headed by a duotone photographic vignette (§5.8) of the customer in context:
  - **Col 1 — Desa 3T:** duotone vignette of a remote eastern-Indonesia household at dusk (kerosene lamp + child studying). Icon: lime energy-node (the village). Top border `#00804C`.
  - **Col 2 — PLN:** duotone vignette of a PLN field technician beside a diesel PLTD shed in the bush. Icon: grid/network node cluster. Top border `#1E488F`.
   - **Col 3 — Investor (dua tier):** duotone vignette of a smartphone showing an OJK-licensed investment app + a small token chip `$AMP` (mikro), plus a faint global-network glyph (institusi). Icon: token chip §5.7. Top border `#7D39EB`. Column holds two stacked relationship-model pills: `B2C · Mikro (mulai Rp2rb, Wadah Bersama)` and `B2B · Institusi Global (likuiditas on-chain, Fase 2)`.
   - Each column: customer name (Plus Jakarta Sans 800, 20 px) + a **relationship-model pill** (Inter 700, 12 px, white on `#001F3F`) stating the B2B/B2G/B2C tag — **Desa 3T → `B2C / B2G` (Penerima Manfaat & Agen)**, **PLN → `B2B / B2G` (Off-taker Utama)**, **Investor → `B2C` (Mikro, mulai Rp2rb) + `B2B` (Institusi Global, on-chain)**. Then the role line, then a **pain-point callout** — a small red-free "bleed" chip: a `#001F3F→#000000` mini meter (status quo cost) with a lime `#C6FF33` gap marker labeled with the pain (e.g. "diesel Rp4.791/kWh", "rugi Rp135rb/RT/bln", "proyek viable sulit diakses", "takut Indo tak transparan"). One pain per customer (investor: satu pain gabungan mikro+global), stated sharply.
  - A thin lime connector drops from each column down to a single shared node at the bottom = "one platform answers all three."
- Background: paper `#F6F7ED` + faint contour motif.

## Speaker notes
> "Siapa pelanggan kami? Bukan satu. Tiga, masing-masing dengan model hubungan berbeda. Pertama, desa tiga-T — sepuluh ribu enam puluh delapan lokasi gelap, andal diesel empat ribu tujuh ratus per kilowatt. Mereka penerima manfaat sekaligus agen: hubungan B2C dan B2G. Kedua, PLN — last-mile mereka merugi seratus tiga puluh lima ribu per rumah per bulan, data titik potensial EBT minim. PLN adalah off-taker utama kami: hubungan B2B dan B2G. Ketiga, investor — dua lapis. Warga mikro mulai dua ribu rupiah lewat Wadah Bersama, hubungan B2C. Dan institusi global yang selama ini ragu karena isu transparansi Indonesia, kami buka lewat likuiditas on-chain yang terverifikasi, hubungan B2B. Tiga pelanggan, empat sisi relasi, satu platform menjawab."
