## Purpose
The ask is no longer the budget (that is Slide 18) — this is the strategic ask: what we need *beyond* the Rp50jt to cross from 1 village to 1,000. Three partnership asks tied to the PLN ICE gates: PLN partnership (offtaker + grid data), OJK pathway (ECF/AKD capital for 100 villages), and ecosystem endorsement to unlock Lisdes / Indonesia Terang alignment. End on the bridge metaphor that opened the deck — bring the arc full circle before the closing cover.
