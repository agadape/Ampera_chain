## Purpose
Show the engine: where money comes in, where it flows, where it lands. Clarify that Ampera earns on a platform fee + diesel-savings margin + carbon share + data services — not by owning generation assets. The model is capital-light and scalable.

## Body copy (text 30%, ≤35 words)
> Ampera bukan pemilik aset. Ampera operasikan **platform investasi terdesentralisasi** — modal warga mikro (Wadah Bersama, mulai Rp2rb) hingga institusi global mengalir ke desa, kilowatt jadi `$AMP` RWA, Ampera ambil biaya layanan + margin penghematan diesel + bagi-hasil karbon + layanan data ESG. Likuiditas on-chain = akses modal masif, transparan, terregulasi. Capital-light, skalabel.
