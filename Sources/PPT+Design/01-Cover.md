# Slide 01 — Cover · "Ampera"

> Archetype A — Hero / Cover. 70% full-bleed visual, 30% wordmark + tagline.

---

## Purpose
Open the deck with one image of the promise: a lit node in a dark archipelago. Establish brand, name, and the one idea — *a bridge of energy*.

## Headline (on-slide)
### ampera
**Jembatan Energi untuk Nusantara.**

## Startup caption (one-line descriptor, ≤18 words)
Inter 500, lime `#C6FF33` @ 90%, 15 px, sits directly under the tagline as the "what is this" line:
> Platform crowd-equity & tokenisasi RWA ($AMP) yang mendanai elektrifikasi desa 3T bersama PLN.

## Eyebrow / meta row
- Left micro-tag: `PLN ICE 2026 · Startup Competition`
- Right micro-tag: `Pitch Deck · Agustus 2026`

## Body copy (text 30%, ≤35 words)
One line under the tagline, Inter 500, white at 80%:
> Menghubungkan modal, teknologi, dan tenaga terbarukan menuju 10.068 desa yang masih gelap — bersama PLN.

## Data to visualize (none numeric here — atmospheric)
- Concept: 1 glowing energy node in a sea of darkness = 1 electrified village among thousands still dark.

## Visual composition (70%)
- **Full-bleed background:** stylized Nusantara archipelago silhouette at night — `linear-gradient(180deg, #000000 0%, #001F3F 70%, #1E488F 100%)`.
- **Topographic contour motif** (§5.3) drawn faintly across the lower third in `#00804C` @ 8%, suggesting land + water.
- **Archipelago:** single-path Indonesia silhouette, hollow, hairline 1.5 px `#74C365` outline, placed lower-center-right, slightly oversized and bleeding off the bottom edge (cinematic).
- **Energy nodes:** ~40 faint dots (`#FFFFFF` @ 25%, 4 px) scattered across the islands = already-electrified points. **One** dominant glowing lime node (`#C6FF33`, 16 px, soft glow `box-shadow 0 0 32px #C6FF33AA`) sits on a far-eastern island — the "one village, lit."
- A single thin lime arc (2 px, `#C6FF33` @ 60%) curves from upper-left "capital/tech" toward that node — the bridge.

## Objects & elements
1. Ampera wordmark (§5.1) — top-left, `ampera` in white, lime node on the second `a`. Plus Jakarta Sans 800, 96 px.
2. Tagline below wordmark, white, 36 px, weight 600.
3. **Startup caption** below tagline, lime `#C6FF33` @ 90%, 15 px — the one-line startup descriptor.
4. Body line, 22 px, white 80%.
5. Meta micro-tags, 13 px uppercase, white 55%, top corners.
5. Archipelago path + node dots (described above).
6. 6 px `#00804C` footer ribbon bottom edge.
7. Slide number `01 / 20` bottom-right, white 40%.

## Color usage
- Bg: black→midnight→nuit gradient.
- Land/contours: `#00804C` / `#74C365` faint.
- Single accent pulse: lime `#C6FF33` (the lit node + bridge arc + wordmark dot) — this is the only "loud" element, by design.

## Typography
- Wordmark: Plus Jakarta Sans 800, 96 px.
- Tagline: Plus Jakarta Sans 600, 36 px.
- Startup caption: Inter 500, 15 px, lime `#C6FF33` @ 90%.
- Body: Inter 500, 22 px.
- Meta: Inter 600 uppercase, 13 px, +0.12em tracking.

## Motion (optional)
- Archipelago + faint dots fade in (0.6s).
- The lime node + bridge arc draw in last (0.8s) and **pulse** gently (§7), 2.5s loop — the only moving element. Everything else still.
