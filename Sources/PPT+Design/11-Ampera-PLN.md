# Slide 11 — Ampera × PLN · Built With PLN, Not Against

> Archetype D+ — 4-layer value stack + partnership scene (70%). Lead: blue. Light slide.

---

## Purpose
Directly answer the jury's implicit question: "does this compete with PLN?" No. Ampera is a force-multiplier for PLN — 4 distinct layers of value that turn PLN's last-mile liability into a partnership asset. Pair the value stack with a real, human partnership scene so the message is not abstract: PLN, the village, the investor, and Ampera stand together on the same bridge. This is the political + human heart of the deck.

## Headline (H2)
### Kami bukan saingan PLN. Kami jembatannya.
