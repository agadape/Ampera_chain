# Slide 08 — AI Tetrix · Empat Agen yang Melihat, Memvalidasi, & Menghargai

> Archetype D — Tech stack diagram (70%). Lead: violet on light. Light slide (paper background).

---

## Purpose
Prove the deep-tech credibility: Ampera doesn't guess where to build — it *sees* the resource from space, *measures* it on the ground, and then an autonomous on-chain agent *values and settles* every kilowatt. This is the moat. Keep it rigorous, not sci-fi. Presented on light/paper to stay consistent with the content-slide family — violet signals "tech" via accent, not via dark cyber background. Now expanded to **four agents (AI Tetrix)**: three for observation/verification (Orbit, Vision, Edge) plus one finance agent (Ledger) that closes the loop.
