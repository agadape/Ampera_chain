# Slide 14 — Market Size · TAM · SAM · SOM

> Archetype E — Concentric market funnel + scale bars (70% visual). Lead: blue. Light slide with dark core.

---

## Purpose
Quantify the market honestly with the classic TAM / SAM / SOM frame the jury expects. Anchor every tier to a real national figure (10.068 desa, Rp50,01 T investasi gap, OJK pool) so the numbers are defensible, not invented. Show Ampera's obtainable slice (1.000 desa by 2028–29) is ambitious yet a fraction of the total — room to grow far beyond the ask.

## Headline (H2)
### Pasarnya lima puluh triliun. Yang kami kejar satu persen.

## Eyebrow
`PASAR · UKURAN POTENSI`

## Body copy (text 30%, ≤40 words)
> **TAM** — 10.068 desa, gap **Rp50,01 T**. **SAM** — ~3.000–4.000 desa (30–40% TAM), ±Rp15–20 T. **SOM** — 100–1.000 desa 2028–29 (**1.000 desa** target), aset **Rp1,5–15 T**, revenue platform **Rp10,4 M/thn**. Kami kejar 1% dulu — sisanya ruang tumbuh.

## Visual composition (70%)
- **Hero object (70%):** a **concentric market funnel with an "acquisition path" piercing inward**, center-right, large — reads as a journey from outer TAM down to the obtainable SOM, not a static donut.
- **3 nested rings (TAM outer → SAM mid → SOM inner):** drawn as a target/donut, top-down. Each ring a different shade — TAM `#1E488F` @ 25% (broadest, lightest), SAM `#1E488F` @ 50%, SOM solid `#1E488F` core with a lime `#C6FF33` ring (the obtainable slice glows).
   - Each ring labeled with its tier name (Inter 700 uppercase, 13 px) + the headline number (Inter 800 tabular, 28 px): TAM `Rp50,01 T` · SAM `±Rp15–20 T` · SOM `1.000 desa · Rp10,4 M/thn`.
  - A small lime wedge cut out of the SOM ring = "1% of TAM" — visualizing how small yet how concentrated the obtainable slice is.
- **Acquisition path (the narrative spine):** a bold lime `#C6FF33` **arrow piercing inward** from the outer-left edge of the TAM ring, through the SAM ring, terminating at the glowing SOM core with a lime "target hit" pop (§5.2 energy-node spark). Labeled along the shaft in 3 stops: `total` (at TAM) → `addressable` (at SAM) → `sekarang` (at SOM). This makes the donut *directional* — "we reach in and grab 1%."
- **Concentric "zoom" depth cue:** the rings cast faint inward drop-shadows (each inner ring slightly raised/elevated) suggesting you're zooming into a smaller, focused target — depth, not flatness.
- **Beside the rings — a vertical scale bar trio:** 3 horizontal bars stacked (TAM longest → SOM shortest), each with its value at the bar end. Bars in `#1E488F` with SOM bar tipped lime — TAM `Rp50,01 T` · SAM `±Rp15–20 T` · SOM `1.000 desa · Rp10,4 M`. Reads left-to-right as "total → reachable → now."
- **Beyond-SOM fade:** the outer ring edge fades into a faint dashed arc labeled "2030+ · 10.000+ desa" — the growth headroom, not part of the ask.
- **Corner micro-pool chips:** 3 tiny chips bottom-right (OJK ECF/AKD pools, Indonesia Terang, 100 GW Solar) — proof the capital & policy back the SOM.
- **Backdrop (behind rings):** a faint Nusantara silhouette ghost at 8% `#00804C` opacity ties the market to geography; faint contour motif behind it.
- Background: paper `#F6F7ED`.

Itu SOM. Satu persen dari total. Dan modalnya sudah ada — OJK buka ECF dan AKD, Indonesia Terang sembilan puluh triliun, seratus gigawatt solar. Kami kejar satu persen dulu. Sisanya, ruang tumbuh."
