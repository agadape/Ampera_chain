# Slide 13 — Unit Economics · The Numbers Per Kilowatt

> Archetype E — Data charts (70%). Lead: midnight. Light slide with dark chart panels.

---

## Purpose
Prove financial kelayakan with hard per-kilowatt economics across all 3 energy types. The jury/investors want to see APY, payback, and carbon revenue in one read. This is the "trust by numbers" slide.

## Headline (H2)
### Angka per kilowatt. Bukan janji per kilowatt.

## Eyebrow
`BISNIS · UNIT ECONOMICS`

## Body copy (text 30%, ≤40 words)
> Solar APY **24,8%**, piko-hidro **23,0%**, biomassa 23,8%. Tambah revenue karbon: hidro ~**Rp387.700/kW/thn**. Break-even ~147 desa. Layak sosial, ya — tapi yang membuat ini bukan amal: layak finansial.

## Data to visualize (chart specs)
**Chart A — APY by energy (horizontal bars):**
- Solar 24,8% (`#74C365`) — longest
- Piko-hidro 23,0% (lime `#C6FF33`)
- Biomassa 23,8% (`#DBE64C`)

**Chart B — CAPEX vs Output scatter (or paired bars):**
- Solar: Rp14–18jt/kW → 97–128 kWh/bln
- Piko-hidro: Rp2–5jt/kW → 200–400 kWh/bln (best quadrant)
- Biomassa: Rp9–90jt/kW → varies

**Chart C — Carbon revenue / kW / thn:**
- Hidro ~Rp387.700
- Solar ~Rp116.600
- (biomassa ~Rp279.700 derived)

**Chart D — Break-even curve:**
- X = #desa, Y = cumulative cash; crosses 0 at ~147 desa (shaded band).

## Visual composition (70%)
- **Hero zone (70%):** a 2×2 grid of compact chart cards (each KPI-card §5.9 framed: white `#FFFFFF` cards on paper `#F6F7ED` bg), so charts read clean and consistent with the light content-slide family.
- A: horizontal bar (APY) — top-left.
- B: scatter/paired bar (CAPEX↔Output) — top-right, piko-hidro quadrant highlighted with lime ring.
- C: vertical bars (carbon rev) — bottom-left.
- D: line crossing zero (break-even) — bottom-right, zero line lime, break-even band `#74C365` 30% fill.
- Each card: micro-title + one-line takeaway under the chart (not legend dump). 1 px `#74C365` top border per card.
- Background: paper `#F6F7ED` throughout + faint contour motif.
