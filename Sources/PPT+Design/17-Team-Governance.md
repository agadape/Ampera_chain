# Slide 17 — Team & Governance · The People Behind the Bridge

> Archetype C — Stage layout with photo placeholders (70%). Lead: midnight. Light slide.

---

## Purpose
Show the team as credible, multi-disciplinary, and properly governed. Present the four founding members on a literal "stage" — circular photo placeholders ready for headshots — so the jury sees real people, not anonymous roles. Pair the human front-stage with a governance back-stage (academic advisor + regulatory awareness) to signal structure that survives beyond the founders.

## Visual composition (70%)
- **Hero object (70%):** a **stage scene**, center-right.
- **Stage floor:** a horizontal rounded platform, `#001F3F` gradient with a soft lime `#C6FF33` front-edge glow (the lit "stage lip"). Slight isometric perspective (front edge wider than back) to give depth — the team stands "on" it. A faint topographic contour motif (§5.3) on the stage top, `#00804C` @ 8%, ties it to the land/Nusantara theme.
- **4 photo placeholders on stage:** 4 large circles (140–160 px diameter) standing in a gentle arc across the stage (not a flat line — an arc = the bridge curve). Each circle:
  - Fill: paper `#F6F7ED` with a faint lime `#C6FF33` dashed border (2 px, dashed) signaling "paste photo here."
  - Centered inside: a small generic person glyph (head + shoulders outline) in `#1E488F` @ 40% — placeholder hint.
  - Below each circle: a **name plate** — a small rounded `#001F3F` chip (stacked, 3 lines): name in white (Inter 700, 14 px), role in lime (Inter 600, 12 px), and a 2-line micro-row in white 70% (Inter 500, 10 px): `[Jurusan · Institusi]` + `[1-line pengalaman kunci]` — placeholder text in `#1E488F` 60% until real bios filled. Plates sit on the stage floor in front of each circle.
  - Order left→right: Hans (CEO), Natalie (CMO), Dave (CTO), Dex (CFO).
- **Backdrop behind the team:** a duotone photographic plate (§5.8, `#001F3F` shadows + `#00804C` highlights) — a soft-focus aerial of a Nusantara village at golden hour, blurred enough to keep the team in focus but carrying the "place" context. The 4 placeholder circles sit in front of this plate.
- **Lime spotlight:** a soft radial gradient (lime `#C6FF33` @ 15% → transparent) from above-center, illuminating the stage — the "spotlight on the team."
- **Below the stage — governance ladder (compact, horizontal):** 3 small linked chips left→right: `Tim inti` (lime) → `Penasihat akademik` (`#74C365`) → `Mitra PLN + regulator` (`#1E488F`), connected by thin `#1E488F` flow-arrows. A small **"+" node** at the right of the ladder labeled *"Penasihat Akademik (validasi riset)"* as a separate, secondary element — not on the main stage (advisor is behind the scenes, validating, not front-stage).
- Background outside the stage area: paper `#F6F7ED` + faint contour motif.
