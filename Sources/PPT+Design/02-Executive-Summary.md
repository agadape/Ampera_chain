# Slide 02 — Executive Summary · One Bridge, One Sentence

> Archetype C+ — Split scene with summary card (70% visual). Lead: midnight + blue. Light slide.

---

## Purpose
Answer the jury's first real question in one frame: *what is Ampera, what field does it work in, and why does it exist?* Condense the whole deck — problem, solution, model — into a single readable summary card paired with the founding vision & mission. This is the "if you remember nothing else, remember this" slide. Sets the strategic frame before the problem deep-dive on Slide 03.

## Headline (H2)
### Satu jembatan. Modal, teknologi, dan terbarukan — menuju desa yang terang.

## Eyebrow
`EXECUTIVE SUMMARY · SIAPA KAMI`

## Body copy (text 30%, ≤55 words)
> **Ampera** adalah platform jembatan energi terbarukan terdesentralisasi. Kami menyatukan **AI site-selection**, **EBT lokal** (surya · piko-hidro · biomassa), **fintech micro-investasi** (OJK ECF/AKD via `$AMP` RWA), dan **akuntansi karbon** untuk menyalakan **10.068 desa** yang masih gelap — bersama PLN, bukan melawannya.
