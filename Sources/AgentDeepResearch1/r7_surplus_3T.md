# Innovative Utilization of Surplus and Off-Grid Renewable Electricity in Remote Indonesian 3T Villages

## Executive Summary

Remote Indonesian 3T villages (Tertinggal/Terluar/Terdepan - disadvantaged/outermost/frontier) face severe energy access challenges, with approximately 2.2 million people lacking electricity and 30 million experiencing unreliable supply. The Indonesian government, primarily through state utility PLN, is pursuing multiple innovative pathways to utilize surplus and off-grid renewable electricity, ranging from green hydrogen micro-hubs to digital value export via tokenization. This analysis examines seven key innovation domains across the technology readiness spectrum.

---

## 1. Green Hydrogen Micro-Hubs and Micro-Ammonia for Diesel Replacement

### What Exists Today
- **PLN operates 22 green hydrogen production facilities** across Indonesia with combined capacity of ~203 tons per year (early 2024)
- Facilities primarily located at geothermal power plants (PLTP Kamojang - Southeast Asia's first) and steam gas power plants (PLTGU Muara Karang, Jakarta)
- **Technology Readiness Level (TRL)**: 5-6 (prototype demonstration in relevant environment)

### What is Piloted
- **Hydrogen Diesel Dual Fuel (H2 DDF) Bus Pilot** on Rote Island (launched July 2026) - demonstrates hydrogen partially replacing fossil fuels in existing diesel bus fleets
- **Green Hydrogen Corridor**: 194-km corridor linking Jakarta-Karawang-Patimban (unveiled July 2026)
- **Diesel Replacement Program (DRP)**: PLN's program to convert ~5,200 diesel power plants (PLTD) to solar PV + BESS, with hydrogen exploration for mini-grids
- **Geothermal-hydrogen system configuration** modeled for Mt Sirung geothermal development

### Future
- Government targeting **nearly 200 tonnes of green hydrogen** market entry in 2026
- **Sumba hydrogen project** targeted for commercial operation in 2028
- Dedicated hydrogen and ammonia government regulation being finalized to secure investment
- Japan and South Korea projected as largest off-takers of Indonesian hydrogen and green ammonia

### Maturity
**Early Pilot to Demonstration Phase (TRL 5-6)**

### Concrete Examples
1. **Kamojang Geothermal Plant**: First green hydrogen production facility in Southeast Asia
   - Source: https://business-indonesia.org/news/green-hydrogen-supply-to-pln-hrs-pltp-kamojang-becomes-the-first-in-southeast-asia

2. **Rote Island H2 DDF Bus**: Modified diesel bus running on hydrogen-diesel dual fuel, reducing CO₂ by 39.37%
   - Source: https://www.globalhydrogenreview.com/hydrogen/23072026/indonesia-accelerates-hydrogen-transport-ecosystem/

3. **PLN's 22 Green Hydrogen Facilities**: Nationwide production capacity of 203 tons/year
   - Source: https://business-indonesia.org/news/government-pushes-hydrogen-to-replace-diesel-sets-price-benchmark-below-local-generation-cost

4. **UK PACT Technical Support**: Simulations of hydrogen interaction with mini-grids on remote Indonesian island model
   - Source: https://www.ukpact.co.uk/sss-initiatives-hydrogren-diesel-replacement-indonesia

## 2. Productive-Use-of-Energy Business Models

### A. Cold Chain

#### What Exists Today
- Solar-powered cold storage units operational in fishing villages (Pacitan, Wakatobi)
- Solar ice makers producing up to 1 ton of ice per day

#### What is Piloted
- **UNDP ACCESS Project**: Solar water pumps and cold chain solutions in 23 villages across 4 provinces (East Nusa Tenggara, West Sulawesi, South-East Sulawesi, Central Kalimantan)
- **Kawa Village Solar Ice Maker** (Maluku, launched Oct 2024): 100% carbon-free, produces up to 1 ton ice/day using thermal energy storage instead of batteries
- **Pertamina NRE Solar Cold Storage**: Planned pilot in two coastal villages in Sumatra (early 2026) with expansion to 82 villages

#### Future
- Scaling solar cold chain to more coastal 3T villages
- Integration with renewable energy mini-grids

#### Maturity
**Pilot to Early Commercial (TRL 7-8)**

#### Concrete Examples
1. **Kawa Village, Maluku**: Solar ice maker transforming small-scale tuna fisheries
   - Source: https://www.undp.org/indonesia/press-releases/launch-solar-ice-maker-kawa-village-boost-indonesian-small-scale-fisheries

2. **Wakatobi and Pacitan**: 20m³ solar-powered cold storage facilities maintaining -2°C for 1000kg fish
   - Source: https://iifiir.org/en/news/solar-powered-cold-storage-for-indonesian-fishing-villages

3. **UNDP ACCESS Project**: 23 villages receiving solar water pumps and cold chain solutions
   - Source: https://www.undp.org/timor-leste/news/game-changer-rural-communities-solar-water-pump-bringing-water-closer-120-households

### B. Agro-Processing

#### What Exists Today

- **Muara Enggelam Village**: Solar PV grid supporting livelihoods in East Kalimantan
- **Anekatek and Akuo Energy mini-grids**: In East Sumba and Berau, increasing productive use of electricity

#### Future
- Scaling productive use appliances and business models
- Integration of solar PV with agricultural processing

#### Maturity
**Pilot to Early Commercial (TRL 7-8)**

#### Concrete Examples

## Key Policy and Market Enablers

1. **Presidential Regulation No. 112/2022**: Mandates replacement of diesel generators with renewable energy across 3T regions
2. **Minister of Energy Regulation No. 19/2025**: Sets pricing framework for renewable energy hybrid systems, requiring hydrogen prices below local diesel costs (USD 0.25-1.19/kWh in remote areas)
3. **Draft Government Regulation on Hydrogen and Ammonia**: Expected to provide legal foundation for investment incentives, business models, and carbon trading integration
4. **PLN's 2060 Net Zero Target**: Driving the Diesel Replacement Program (5,200 PLTD units)
5. **UNDP Ocean Innovation Challenge**: Providing up to $250,000 for sustainable fisheries innovation

---

## Critical Gaps and Challenges

1. **Micro-ammonia**: No identified pilots or commercial deployments in Indonesia's 3T villages; remains at academic research level
2. **Agrivoltaics**: Limited to research phase with no deployed pilots in 3T regions
3. **P2P Energy Trading**: Still in stakeholder perception and framework development stage; no operational village cluster trading systems identified
4. **RWA Tokenization**: Conceptual stage with no identified projects specifically for 3T village energy assets
5. **Off-grid E-Mobility**: Limited to hydrogen bus pilot on Rote Island; no electric vehicle charging infrastructure in 3T villages identified

The most mature and deployed solutions are solar PV + BESS mini-grids with productive uses (cold chain, agro-processing, water pumps), while hydrogen and digital energy trading remain in earlier development stages with promising pilot initiatives.
