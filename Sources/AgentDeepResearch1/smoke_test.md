## QUESTION

Smoke test: what is PLN's current renewable energy capacity target in Indonesia?

## ANSWER


Based on the latest Electricity Supply Business Plan (RUPTL) 2025-2034, PLN's current renewable energy capacity targets are:

**By 2030:** 18.6 GW of renewable power capacity (reduced from the previous RUPTL 2021-2030 target of 20.9 GW)

**By 2034:** 42.6 GW of total renewable energy capacity, accompanied by 10.3 GW of energy storage

The 42.6 GW target by 2034 represents the primary long-term goal in PLN's current planning framework. This capacity is expected to be primarily composed of:
- Solar: 17.1-17.2 GW
- Hydropower: 11.7 GW  
- Wind: 7.2 GW
- Geothermal: 5.2 GW
- Bioenergy: 0.9 GW
- Nuclear: 0.5 GW (planned for Sumatra and Kalimantan)

The plan also outlines a phased implementation approach, with 12.2 GW of new renewable capacity targeted for 2025-2029 and 30.4 GW for 2030-2034.

It's worth noting that while these are PLN's official targets under the RUPTL 2025-2034, Indonesia's progress has been slow—as of April 2025, PLN had only realized 1.6 GW of renewable energy capacity, representing just 7.6% of the previous 20.9 GW target. Additionally, there have been separate government proposals (such as the 100 GW solar plan announced in August 2025) that have not yet been integrated into PLN's official RUPTL targets.
